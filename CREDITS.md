# Credits

New Blitz 2027 is a Blitz Recomp project. It builds on other people's work:

| Who | What |
|---|---|
| **Midway Games** | NFL Blitz 2000 (1999). This project contains none of the game's assets; you supply your own cartridge dump. |
| **Wiseguy (Mr-Wiseguy) and contributors** | [N64Recomp](https://github.com/N64Recomp/N64Recomp) (MIT), N64ModernRuntime (GPL-3.0), and the Zelda64Recomp UI that RecompFrontend grew from |
| **The RT64 contributors** | [RT64](https://github.com/rt64/rt64), the renderer (MIT) |
| **Zoinkity** | The Team Extension, whose research showed a 32-team Blitz was possible |
| **The NFL Blitz Resurrection team** (ScallyCap, UltraMagnus, Avery, vaycant, DJSteve067) | Years of community updates that kept Blitz current |
| **jaker3 and nflblitzfans.com** | Keeping the Blitz hacking community going |
| **SDL, FreeType, RmlUi, lunasvg, miniz, nlohmann/json, qrcodegen, Monocypher** | Libraries the app uses (each one's licence: NOTICES.md and `lib/`) |

The New Blitz 2027 logo, the app's UI design and the credits page are the Blitz Recomp team's. The 2026 Season pak's
team art and uniforms were drawn for the project; the pak is a separate download and isn't in this repository.
