Getting started

From download to kickoff

Get New Blitz for your computer. The first run takes a minute, and then the computer sets up the rest: your phone, handheld, PS5 or a real N64, with your ROM and your setup already in.

First run

Download the app for your computer from the download page and open it (the first time, your computer asks: here's how). Then:

Prefer to watch? Setup on a Mac (video) or on Windows (video), start to finish.

  1. Where do you want to play? This Computer; Phone or Handheld (an Android device or an iPhone, set up with your ROM and your setup); PS5; or N64 or Emulator, a ROM with your setup built in. The rest of this page has each one.
  2. Load ROM. Pick your dump of NFL Blitz 2000 (USA), v1.1 or v1.0 (the app updates a v1.0 dump to v1.1): a .z64, .v64 or .n64, or a .zip holding one. The app checks it's the right cartridge and keeps its own copy, so you can move the original afterwards.
  3. Add the 2026 season? Add It downloads the 2026 Season pak (today's teams, rosters, uniforms and team art, about 2 MB) and plays New Blitz with it. Just the 1999 Game plays the cartridge exactly as it shipped: Original 1999. Either way you get widescreen, save states and the rest, and you can add or remove the season later under Add-Ons. More about the season.
  4. Keep rosters up to date? If you added the season: Roster and uniform updates are on by default: the app downloads new lineups (injuries, signings, depth charts) and uniforms when it starts. Turn them off any time in Settings → Teams → Keep Rosters and Uniforms Up to Date.
  5. Set it up for you? Recommended plays straight away. Customize opens the house rules (Receiver Buttons, the play clock, realism and more), all off until you turn them on.

From then on the app opens straight into the game. Settings → General → Blitz Builder runs the Builder again whenever you want another device or an export, and Settings → Saves → Reset Data starts over from the first run (it can keep your ROM and back everything up first, to Documents → New Blitz Backups). The first time you reach each part of the game, a small tip card shows what to press, in your own controller's buttons. Playing on more than one device? Settings → Saves → Cloud Sync carries your season and records across (how it works).

Playing on your computer

  • Gamepads work out of the box, over USB or Bluetooth: PlayStation, Xbox, Switch Pro and others. Each controller becomes the next free player, 1 to 4, and shows its player's colour. Remap buttons in Settings → Controls.
  • Phones as controllers: Settings → Players → Phone Controllers. Phones on the same Wi-Fi scan the QR code, turn sideways and join as the next player. Turn it off when you're done: anyone on your network can join while it's on. On Windows, the first time, the firewall asks about New Blitz: click Allow access, or phones can't connect.
  • Settings → Players shows who is who: Identify buzzes a controller, Swap trades two players' places, Make Player 1 hands over the menus.
  • On a TV: Settings → Graphics → TV Mode goes full screen with a widescreen picture in one go.
  • Player 1 can use the keyboard: see Controls.

iPhone and iPad

Status: Preview. A free Apple ID works.

Apple only lets you install apps like this one if you sign them yourself, with your own Apple ID. Nobody else's certificate or name is involved, and it's for your own devices. We recommend Sideloadly, on a Mac or Windows. It can also refresh the app for you over the same Wi-Fi.

Your Apple IDHow long it runsGood to know
Free7 days at a timeWith AutoRefresh on, Sideloadly renews it for you. Your saves stay. Up to 3 sideloaded apps per device.
Paid ($99 a year)A yearThe Apple Developer Program. No 3-app limit.
  1. Install Sideloadly. On Windows it also needs Apple's iTunes and iCloud from apple.com (the web versions, not the Microsoft Store ones).
  2. Plug the iPhone in with a cable.
  3. Drag NewBlitz.ipa into Sideloadly and sign in with your own Apple ID. A free one works. You type the password into Sideloadly. It's never shared with New Blitz.
  4. Before you start, turn on AutoRefresh: the circular-arrows button to the left of Start.
  5. On the iPhone: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → trust your Apple ID. Turn on Developer Mode if iOS asks.
  6. Turn on Wi-Fi sync for the phone. On a Mac: Finder → select the iPhone → "Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi". On Windows: the same option in iTunes.
  7. Open New Blitz. It starts ready to play.

Make NewBlitz.ipa on your computer first: Blitz Builder → Phone or Handheld → iPhone → Save the iPhone App (.ipa). It has your ROM and your setup inside, and lands in Downloads → New Blitz for iPhone. The .ipa on the release page has no ROM: installed by hand, it asks you to set it up from your computer, or to Pick a File Instead (or share the ROM to New Blitz from the Files app).

Keeping it going

A free Apple ID's install lasts 7 days. With AutoRefresh on, Sideloadly re-signs it in the background every few days, whenever the computer is on and the phone is on the same Wi-Fi. There is nothing to click, and your saves stay. If it ever lapses, the app won't open until it's refreshed: plug in and click Start again in Sideloadly. Don't delete the app to fix it, because that deletes its saves. You'll need iOS 17 or later.

Other options

  • AltStore with AltServer also refreshes over Wi-Fi from a computer.
  • SideStore refreshes on the phone itself, but setup is fiddlier.

Prefer to build it yourself with Xcode? The step-by-step guide is in the repository: docs/IOS_SIDELOAD.md.

Android

Phones and handhelds running Android 10 or later on a 64-bit ARM chip with Vulkan graphics: that's most from the last several years. The Builder installs the app with your ROM and setup already in it.

  1. Turn on USB debugging. On the device: Settings → About phone (on some devices it's under Software information), tap Build number seven times to unlock Developer options, then turn on USB debugging in them.
  2. Install platform-tools (adb) on your computer, from Android's developer site.
  3. Connect the device with a USB cable and allow debugging when it asks.
  4. In the computer app: Blitz Builder → Phone or Handheld → Android → Send over USB. It installs New Blitz, with your ROM, your settings and the season if you added it, and the device starts ready to play.

No adb? Save the APK saves the app with your ROM and setup beside it, to copy over yourself. Installed the .apk from the release page by hand instead? Open it and choose Pick a File Instead.

Play with the on-screen touch controls, a Bluetooth gamepad, or a handheld's own buttons. Select (or the device's Back button) opens the menu. Screenshots and saved replays go to Pictures → New Blitz, where your gallery finds them.

Dual-screen handhelds Beta

It's the same Android app. On a device with two screens, it finds the second one when it starts and uses both: BLITZ DS.

  • During a play, the field runs tall across both screens, with a camera that follows the action.
  • At play select, the game's own play tiles are tappable on one screen, while the other loops the last play beside the score and your drive.
  • Stylus (Settings → DS → DS Screens): the game on one screen and big tiles on the other, with Receiver Buttons on for player 1.
  • Settings → DS has Two Screens (on by default), DS Screens (Both, Companion, Stylus, Controller), Swap Screens, the camera and a Custom Layout. On a Surface Duo, spanned, it is a two-screen DS too; wide upper screens (Retroid Pocket Duo, AYN Thor) fill with field, tested at their sizes on a Mac only. Two-Screen Sharpness (Settings → Graphics → Potato Mode) is 1.75x by default on an RG DS and can go from 1x to 2x.

It's in beta: it has run on two-screen handhelds, and how smoothly depends on the device. Tell us how yours does on Discord.

PS5 Preview

New Blitz runs as native homebrew on a jailbroken PS5 running etaHEN. We can't help with jailbreaking a console; which firmware versions work is up to etaHEN.

  1. Start etaHEN on the console, and have the PS5 and your computer on the same network.
  2. Find the console's address (its IP address, in the PS5's network settings).
  3. In the computer app: Blitz Builder → PS5, and type the address. The Builder sends the game, your ROM and your setup over etaHEN's FTP server, and starts it.

It shows up on the home screen like any title, and plays with a DualSense. The game's files (the ROM, logs, exports) are in /data/blitzrecomp on the console. The Roster Room and phone controllers show their addresses for a phone or computer to open.

A real N64, or an emulator

Export ROM (Blitz Builder → N64 or Emulator) saves a cartridge-ready .z64 to your Downloads folder, with your rosters, rules and uniforms built in. Put it on a flash cart for a real Nintendo 64, or open it in any N64 emulator. Each export is a new file with the date and time in its name. With a league pak chosen (the CFL or the Pac-12), it makes a cartridge for Arcade and Tournament.

The Lite, Full and season kinds need the 2026 Season pak. Without it, Export ROM offers Original 1999 and Custom. Optional extras ride along from your settings: Choose CPU Opponent, Play Clock, the Blitz codes, Home Field and Realism, among others. When it's done, it lists what went in.

Controls

Buttons go by position, so every pad plays the same way: the bottom face button passes, the left one jumps and tackles. The game's own screens show each player their own controller's buttons.

The extras

On a controller, hold the hotkey, R3 (the right stick pressed in; L3 too on one-stick handhelds like the RG DS), and press the button. While it's held, the game doesn't see those presses.

On handhelds whose L1/R1 are the turbo buttons, saving and loading move to hotkey + R2 and L2. In the app's menus: ✕ / A selects, □ or ○ goes back, L1 / R1 change tabs; on a keyboard, Enter, Backspace and Page Up / Page Down. On a Mac, Shift+F12 records a clip.
