How it works

Three steps to kickoff

  1. 1

    Download the Builder

    One free app for your Mac, Windows PC or Linux machine. It's the game, and the Builder that sets it up everywhere else.

  2. 2

    Load your own ROM

    Point it at your dump of NFL Blitz 2000 (USA) v1.1. It checks it's the right cartridge and keeps a copy. Nothing from the game comes in the download.

  3. 3

    Play here or send it

    Play on this computer, or let the Builder put the game on your phone, handheld or PS5, ready to play. Or write a cartridge-ready file for a real N64.

The full getting-started guide

Multiplayer, the way Blitz was meant to be played

Four players. No extra controllers

Everyone's phone is a controller. Open Settings → Players, and friends on the same Wi-Fi scan the QR code, turn their phone sideways and they're in. Mix phones with gamepads, up to four players on one screen.

Everything for game night
1

Phones as pads

Scan, turn sideways, play. A stick on the left, Pass, Jump and Turbo on the right, buttons labelled for what's on screen.

2

Who's who

Each controller lights up in its player's colour. Identify buzzes one, Swap trades places, Make Player 1 hands over the menus.

3

Tournaments

Brackets for 2 to 8 named players, one game or best of three, saved after every game and back after a restart.

4

Blitz Party

Quick Setup → Party in one pick: infinite turbo, late hits, the big football. TV Mode goes full screen and widescreen at once.

What's new around the game

The game as it was, plus everything since

Do-overs

Botched a kickoff? Run it back.

  • Redo Play rewinds to play select, up to 8 plays back, score, clock and stats with it. In the menus it undoes a wrong team pick.
  • Save states: three slots and an Auto slot, mid-play included.
  • Instant Replay: scrub the last play, slow it down, save a clip.
  • Save & Quit mid-game, and pick it back up next time.

Football smarts

House rules you can switch on, all off until you want them.

  • Receiver Buttons: a button icon floats over each receiver. Press it to throw to him.
  • Realism: 10-yard first downs, touchbacks at the 25, extra points that can miss, overtime.
  • Play Clock, a Smart CPU opponent and Smarter Play Calling.

The 2026 season, one click

An optional, free add-on: today's 32 teams, rosters, schedule, uniforms and team art, about 2 MB. Weekly lineups and uniforms keep it current, and you can turn that off. Don't want it? Original 1999 is always there, exactly as it shipped.

About the 2026 Season

Back to the cartridge

Export ROM writes a cartridge-ready .z64 with your rosters, rules and uniforms built in, for a flash cart on a real Nintendo 64 or any emulator. Lite plays on any N64; Full uses the Expansion Pak.
