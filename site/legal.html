Credits and legal

Standing on shoulders

New Blitz 2027 is a Blitz Recomp project. It builds on years of other people's work, and it's open source for the same reason.

The New Blitz 2027 logo, the app's interface design and the credits page are the Blitz Recomp team's. The 2026 Season pak's team art and uniforms were drawn for the project; the pak is a separate, optional download.
