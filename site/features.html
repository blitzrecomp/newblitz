Features

Everything worth knowing

At its heart it's NFL Blitz 2000, the cartridge's own game, running as native code. Around it, everything a modern setup wants. The extras that change the rules are off until you turn them on. Watch the feature reel (video).

One core, every device

Runs everywhere

Native, not emulated

The game's code was translated ahead of time into native code for each device. Your ROM supplies the rest: the graphics, the sound, the game's data. It starts fast and runs light.

Two ways to play

Original 1999 is the cartridge exactly as it shipped. New Blitz adds the optional 2026 Season. Every feature on this page works in both, apart from the ones that need the season.

Mac, Windows (preview), Linux, iPhone and iPad, Android, dual-screen Android (beta), PS5 (jailbroken), and a real N64 through Export ROM. How to set each one up.

Same game, sharper

Looks

Hi-res and widescreen

The 1999 game drawn at your screen's resolution, and in widescreen.

60 fps or more

Smooth on anything from a handheld to a big TV.

TV Mode

Settings → Graphics → TV Mode: full screen with a widescreen picture, in one switch, for the couch.

The centrepiece

Multiplayer

Blitz was always a party game. Now everyone brings their own controller: the phone in their pocket.

1

Phones as controllers

Settings → Players → Phone Controllers. Phones on the same Wi-Fi scan the QR code, turn sideways and join as the next free player: a stick on the left, Pass, Jump and Turbo on the right, Start at the top, labelled for what's on screen. The ··· menu has save, load, Redo Play and Be player 1. A phone keeps its place if it locks for a moment.

2

Up to four players

Phones, gamepads and a keyboard, mixed any way you like. Each controller becomes the next free player and shows its colour: blue 1, red 2, green 3, yellow 4. Settings → Players shows who is who: Identify buzzes a controller, Swap trades places, Make Player 1 hands over the menus.

3

Tournaments

Settings → Teams has a Bracket section that sets up a bracket for 2 to 8 named players: one game a round or best of three, random matchups or as typed. Teams are picked before each game. The bracket saves after every game and comes back after a restart; Show Standings has everyone's record.

4

Blitz Party

Rules → Quick Setup → Party: infinite and faster turbo, late hits, a big football and field goal odds, in one pick. Add TV Mode and phone controllers and game night's set up in a minute.

Run it back

Do-overs

Redo Play

Botched a kickoff? Hotkey + △ (or F8) goes back to that play's play select, with the score, clock, down and stats rewound. Press again at play select to go back another play, up to 8. In the menus it undoes a step instead: picked the wrong team? Back you go.

Instant Replay

The last play, full screen, with the game stopped behind it: play and pause, scrub frame by frame, slow it to half or quarter speed, and save it. Open it from the pause menu's REPLAY, F9, or R1 at play select.

Save states

Save the game exactly as it is, mid-play included, in three slots, and load it back later, even after quitting. A fourth slot, Auto, fills itself when the app goes to the background mid-game. A note at the bottom right says what happened.

Save & Quit

Quitting mid-game asks Save this game? Next time, Pick up your game? puts you straight back where you were.

House rules for football heads

Football smarts

All in Settings → Rules, all off until you want them.

Receiver Buttons

When your quarterback has the ball, a button icon floats over each receiver and follows his route. Press it to throw to him. Hold Turbo before the snap to see who's where. On a phone, small arrow buttons wrap around Pass.

Realism

Pro-style rules, each starting at the original game's: first downs at 30, 20 or 10 yards; kickoff touchbacks at the 20, 25 or 35; two-point tries from the 10 or the 2; extra points that can miss; overtime periods up to three, or one, then a tie.

Play Art

Hold Turbo at the line and your receivers' routes are painted on the turf the way the playbook draws them, in their Receiver Buttons' colours. Settings → Rules → Play Art: Off, First Break or Full.

Play Clock

Normal (9 seconds in the first quarter, 5 after), or 15 or 20 seconds every quarter.

Smart CPU Opponent

The game's own Smart CPU, on for every one-player game: the computer plays at its top skill. Tougher, not smarter.

Smarter Play Calling

The CPU kicks the field goal at the end of a half, doesn't try field goals over 55 yards, fakes far less, and goes for two only late.

Choose CPU Opponent

Pick who you play against in a one-player game.

Fun

House rules

The Blitz codes

Big Heads, Tiny Players, Infinite Turbo, weather and stadiums, on for every game. No code entry at the versus screen needed.

Quick Setup

Original, Party or Tournament rules in one pick, plus any rules files friends have shared.

Share your rules

Rules → Save Setup to File saves your house rules as a file. Send it to a friend; it shows up in their Quick Setup.

The optional add-on

2026 Season

With the free 2026 Season pak (more about it).

32 teams, today

Today's teams in today's cities, with 2026 rosters, current uniforms and team art. Advanced Season plays the real 2026 schedule with 7-team playoffs.

Pick Your Crew

After team select, pick who plays for your team: a QB, RB, WR and a flex, then the line and the defense, and the CPU's crew too. Looks only: names, numbers and builds, never ratings.

Uniform Picker

At team select, △ cycles your team's uniform: home, away, or the game's choice. The other team adapts so the two don't clash.

Pro Bowl

At team select, the game's R button makes an all-star crew from every team's starters, with your own three-letter team code on the scoreboard.

League paks

Play the CFL or the Pac-12, with their 2026 rosters, logos, helmets, end zones and every uniform, in Arcade and Tournament. Get CFL 2026 and Pac-12 2026 in Settings → Add-Ons, then browse with ◀ ▶ under Settings → Teams → League and press × to choose (the game restarts to load them).

Redone uniforms for all 32 teams

All 32 teams' home, away and alternate sets (116 choices) were redrawn and checked on the field, with new matchup-screen helmets and corrected end zones. It's a 2026 Season pak update, so it reaches every version of New Blitz.

Weekly lineups

Who's starting this week, injuries and all, plus current uniforms, downloaded when the app starts. On by default; turn it off in Settings → Teams. Lineups is Current (Week XX) or All Starters (Healthy).

Roster Room

A page in your browser with every team's slots and the whole roster to choose from. Lock In, and your crews play in every mode and go into exported ROMs.

In your hands

Handhelds

BLITZ DS Beta

On a two-screen handheld, the play runs tall across both screens with a camera that follows the action. At play select, tap a play on one screen while the other loops the last play beside your drive. Stylus puts big tiles under your thumb, and Controller turns the lower screen into a touch controller. Settings → DS has the modes, the camera and a Custom Layout with a slider for every piece. Two screens are on by default, and Two-Screen Sharpness (1.75x by default on an RG DS) trades a little speed for a crisper picture.

Surface Duo and wide upper screens

On a Surface Duo, spanned, the game is a two-screen DS: the field across both, play select and your thumbs below. On wide upper screens like the Retroid Pocket Duo and AYN Thor, the top screen fills with field instead of black bars. The wide screens are tested at their sizes on a Mac only so far, so tell us how it plays on the real thing.

Touch controls

On a phone, a floating stick on the left, Pass, Jump, Turbo and R on the right, Start and the menu a tap away. They step aside when a controller's in use.

Hotkey combos

Hold R3 (the right stick pressed in) for the extras: save, load, pick a slot, Redo Play, screenshots. On one-stick handhelds, L3 works too. The same on every controller. All the controls.

Your game, your way

Make it yours

Export ROM

A cartridge-ready .z64 with your rosters, rules and uniforms built in, for a flash cart on a real N64 or any emulator. Lite plays on any N64; Full uses the Expansion Pak; Custom is your own mix. A league pak exports too, for Arcade and Tournament. The kinds.

Cloud Sync

Play a season on your phone and pick it up on your handheld or computer. Settings → Saves → Cloud Sync: turn it on, then on your other device choose Join and type the 6-digit number the first one shows. It syncs your Controller Pak and season saves, not save states. Saves are encrypted before they leave your device, and there's no account. If two devices disagree, it shows both and asks; nothing is replaced behind your back.

Sync over Wi-Fi, or by file

Settings → Saves → Sync over Wi-Fi sends your saves straight to another device on the same network. Export / Import Save File makes a file you can move yourself, by Drive, AirDrop or a USB stick.

Reset Data

Settings → Saves → Reset Data starts over from the first run. Back Up and Reset saves everything to a zip first, Keep my ROM spares your cartridge file, and Restore a Backup brings it all back. Settings → Saves → Backups keeps dated copies of your Controller Pak too.

Add-Ons

Paks add seasons, uniform sets, team art, announcer calls and rules. Add-Ons installs them, updates them and removes them; paks you've added update themselves when the app starts, Check looks right now, and Open Folder works on phones too. Community paks are shared on Discord.

The little things

Polish

First-run hints

The first time you reach each part of the game, a tip card shows what to press, in your own controller's buttons. Each shows once and never blocks a button.

Your buttons, everywhere

The game's own screens show each player their own controller's buttons: PlayStation, Xbox or Switch. Or pick one style, the N64's included. Settings → Players → Buttons sets a single controller's.

On-screen keyboard

Type tournament names or a team code with a controller.

Screenshots and clips

F12 or hotkey + Select for a screenshot; on a Mac, Shift+F12 records a clip. They go to Pictures → New Blitz.

Toasts

Small notes at the bottom right say what just happened, like "Slot 1: state saved", then get out of the way.

Bug reports in one click

Settings → General → Save Logs puts the log, any crash reports and your device details in one zip. Attach it to a bug report. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on.
