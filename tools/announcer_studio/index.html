Announcer Studio

Houston and Washington are silent in the 2026 app because the original game has no recordings for them, and the Raiders, Chargers and Rams still say Oakland, San Diego and St. Louis. Here you record short calls in your own voice; the builder trims them, takes them down to the game's 8.75 kHz voice and matches the tone and loudness of the original calls.

1 · What a team bank is

Every team has the same four calls. Pick a team to hear the game's own reads (decoded from your ROM, nothing saved).

CallWhen the game says it
  • Short and punchy. The game's calls last about half a second to a second; it says a name between other lines.
  • Shout a little, from a hand's width away. The builder compresses hard, so energy matters more than volume.
  • Three or four takes each, then keep the best one. Takes stay in this page until you keep one.
  • A quiet room, laptop mic is fine. Everything above 4 kHz is cut anyway, like the original.
  • Your own voice only, or one you have the rights to.
